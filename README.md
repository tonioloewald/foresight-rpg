# ForeSight RPG
<!--{ "pin": "top", "order": 0, "headTitle": "ForeSight RPG — a streamlined tabletop RPG", "description": "A modern edition of the ForeSight RPG: one resolution rule (Score × Ease Factor), lethal fast combat, and a careful generative magic system." }-->

A streamlined, modern edition of the **ForeSight** tabletop RPG, built on the unpublished 2004 second edition and the 1986 original.

**One rule:** Success Chance = Score × Ease Factor — roll D100 under it. Everything else is that rule applied to different situations.

Use the navigation to browse the rules one-pagers, or open the live **[Character Builder](/character-builder/)**.

## This is work-in-progress

If you have a problem with these rules [file an issue](https://github.com/tonioloewald/foresight-rpg/issues). But remember, the goal of ForeSight is to be "all the rules you can remember", so producing big lists of stuff or complex definitive procedures for things (like Aftermath's bullet impact resolution flowchart, or Universe's wilderness encounter system, or even RuneQuest 3's Dropped Oil Lamp Table) is not the point.

You, the GM or player, have better insight into the weird special case at hand than I or any game designer can ever possibly have. Use [the one rule](/the-one-rule/) and get on with the game.
