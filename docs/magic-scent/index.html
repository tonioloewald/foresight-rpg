Scent

created and illusory smells and tastes (formerly Odours & Tastes)

A Fundamental — a Field of Magical Knowledge whose applications reach 50m and last 10min; the usual props are perfume, musk, or incense. Learn it Basic (1 yr) for the easy end, Advanced (3 yr) for the deep.
