Earth

command earth, stone, and metal

A Fundamental — a Field of Magical Knowledge whose applications reach 10m and last 10min; the usual props are a piece of intrusive igneous rock (eg. granite), and. Learn it Basic (1 yr) for the easy end, Advanced (3 yr) for the deep.

Protection against Earth

Intensity
2
Code
§8A5
Source
earth

The invoker may protect him/herself, or one target, against the damaging effects of cold and drying, to the effect of reducing all such attacks causing damage through these qualities by 4 DCs, and by 2 DCs if they are among the effects involved (fixed, bestowed).

Intensity
2
Code

The invoker may create a cone of withering (this is a drying attack) with its base anywhere within range. The cone is 6m wide at its base and draws to a point at its invoker. Those wholly or mostly within it suffer DC3 from drying (immediate).
