Air

command and conjure air, wind, and gases

A Fundamental — a Field of Magical Knowledge whose applications reach 40m and last 2min; the usual props are a fan (or perhaps even an ardent admirer). Learn it Basic (1 yr) for the easy end, Advanced (3 yr) for the deep.

Conjure Air

Intensity
0
Code
§2B1
Source
air

Create a 5m×5m×5m volume of air in a space perhaps already occupied by air or vapour (but nothing denser). This volume may be decreased, but the air may not be compressed further. Used judiciously, this spell creates gusts of wind, etc. (immediate).

Dormitive Vapours

Intensity
1
Code
§2A5
Source
air

Create a volume of anaesthetic vapour occupying a volume 1m×1m×1m (fixed). Inhalers must make an EF=5 WP roll to resist falling asleep, and remain asleep until the gas disperses and the inhaler has regained reserve fatigue levels, or is shaken awake. Note: a character or creature whose fatigue is already at or above reserve fatigue will only remain asleep until the gas disperses or he/she is shaken awake.

The invoker may render him/herself, or one recipient, capable of surviving in a hostile (or lacking) atmosphere for the duration of the spell (controlled, bestowed).

Protection from Air

Intensity
1
Code
§2B7
Source
air

The invoker may render him/herself or another more resistant to attacks which damage through heat or moistening, by reducing the DCs of said attacks by 4. If the attack damages through one of the two, and something else (eg. drying, like a fire-based attack), then this protection drops to a reduction in DC of 2.

The invoker may create a cone of heat with its base anywhere within range. The cone is 20m wide at its base and draws to a point at its invoker. Those wholly or mostly within it suffer DC3 from heat (immediate).

Lightning Bolt

Intensity
3
Code
§2A12
Source
air

Create a lightning bolt 1m wide extending from the invoker to a point within range (immediate). Anyone touched by the lightning bolt, or placed in electrical contact with it, takes DC6 and is automatically stunned. If he/she achieves a QR10 on his/her PR roll (for the damage) he/she dies of heart failure, and must be revived by a skilled person (at least if in low-tech surroundings) within a minute. A lightning bolt damages through heat and moisture.
