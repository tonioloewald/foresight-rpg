Sound

created and illusory sound (formerly Sounds)

A Fundamental — a Field of Magical Knowledge whose applications reach 50m and last 10min; the usual props are a musical instrument. Learn it Basic (1 yr) for the easy end, Advanced (3 yr) for the deep.
