Wood

command plants, wood, and living growth

A Fundamental — a Field of Magical Knowledge whose applications reach 20m and last 10min; the usual props are a green branch, a small piece of fresh mistletoe,. Learn it Basic (1 yr) for the easy end, Advanced (3 yr) for the deep.

Expand a wooden object ≤2m×2m×2m by increasing its linear dimensions by up to 100%. The object's mass remains constant, so that normal wood would become as light as balsa, while its strength is unchanged.

Contract Wood

Intensity
1
Code
§25B3
Source
wood

Contract a wooden object ≤2m×2m×2m by reducing its linear dimensions by up to 50%. The object's mass remains constant, while its strength and density increase markedly. - If armour is made out of such wood, it will be as metal armour of the equivalent type, but with protection values one worse in each category. - A weapon normally made of metal may be made of this material, but will have its DCs reduced by one. - A weapon normally made of wood may be made of this material, in which case its DCs are improved by one, while its mass is increased by 50%.
