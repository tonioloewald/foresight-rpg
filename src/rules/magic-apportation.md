Rays of light, sounds, and inanimate objects (unless carried or pushed by a creature) do not pass through. <strong>Optional Teleport &amp; Gate Rules.</strong> Applications of Apportation marked with an asterisk (*) receive the following modifications to their intensity: - Do not change the intensity if the origin is extremely similar in both form and function to the destination. (Eg. teleporting from one plush throne-room to another throne room of similar appearance; or opening a gate between two stone doorways of similar size and construction.) - Add one to the intensity if the origin is similar in either shape or form to the destination. (Eg. teleporting to a throne room from a barn decorated so as to resemble it; or opening a gate between a stone doorway leading into a corridor and another opening into an alcove.) - Add two to the intensity if the origin and destination are not radically different. (Eg. teleporting from an open field to a grassy knoll.) This also applies if the origin and destination are not very distinct locations — that is, land-marks or easily named locations. A room would be more &quot;distinct&quot; than a corridor, a crossroad more so than a road, a hill more so than a field. - Add three (or more) to the intensity if the origin and destination are radically different. (Eg. teleporting from the middle of a desert to the shore of a lake — you might add four to the intensity.)</p></section></div></div>
