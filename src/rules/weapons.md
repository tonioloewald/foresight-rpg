# Weapons
<!--{ "parent": "world", "order": 3 }-->

*A weapon is an [object](/equipment/) with combat stats. The list is deliberately short — one noun carries many real items as adjectives (a **carbine** is a *light* rifle, a **sawn-off** is a *cut-down* shotgun), so add variety with a modifier, not a new row.*

**Damage Class (DC)** is the number that meets the [Damage](/damage/) table; the whole scale is anchored to three points — a **very strong person's punch is 4**, a **9mm pistol is 8**, a **7.62 rifle is 11**. **PM** is the ease-factor bonus for using it as intended. **Damage type** is **M**echanical (cutting), **I**mpact (crushing), or **B**urning/ballistic; a trailing **P** means it *pierces* armor (which armor resists with its own **A**bsorb). **Range** is short–long in metres (melee weapons give reach); **RoF** in brackets is the automatic burst size.

> A weapon is only a benchmark, not a licence: in combat, magic and gear alike are judged against *what a good archer does without either*. That's why the humble Bow sits on this list as a yardstick.
