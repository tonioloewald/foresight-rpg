The sandestin may use skills involving IN, and may be given (up to three years' worth of) fields of knowledge possessed by the invoker, instead of a skill. The sandestin may perform magical tasks if it has a source of fatigue to pay for them.

These sandestins have one major drawback, however: since they resent forced servitude, they will pervert the spirit, but not the letter, of any command they are given, whenever possible.

Note: if an invoker creates a magical effect containing an intensity 3 or 5 application of Sandestin, then his/her trained IN is reduced by one for its duration (which may be permanent). Lost IN and skill levels are restored when the effect stops (if the effect is obliterated — eg. by Instil — then the loss is permanent). For every such sandestin the invoker already (currently) has in operation (ie. as part of an extant effect) he/she incurs a -1 modifier to all IN, and IN-related (using fields of knowledge, or IN-dependent skills), tasks.

If the target is non-sapient, then the invoker controls the organism (assuming it fails to resist) completely, extinguishing its &quot;soul&quot;. However, while in the body of a non-sapient organism, the invoker increases the intensity of any effect he/she attempts to invoke by two for incantations, and by four for rituals (on top of modifiers for lacking props); casting is unaffected.

If the target is sapient, then the personality with lower WP (the more original personality, in the case of equal WPs) is suppressed whenever the stronger is awake — except where the stronger personality attempts something greatly contrary to the interests of the weaker, in which case both make EF5 WP rolls, the better QR prevailing (a tie indicating the target's body is stunned for fifteen seconds or so, at which point another roll is made). A QR10 WP roll made in such circumstances causes the demise of the fumbling personality. A body left with no personality dies.

A magician's mind in an inanimate object which is not a source of fatigue (such as a staff or fatigue battery) won't be able to produce any effects, even by casting. Note that this application does not produce a &quot;sandestin&quot; as such. (Range 20m; Duration: instantaneous.)

Notes: this application requires a human brain as a prop. If transplanted into a body, this sandestin can use its mental skills, and its physical skills to a degree permitted by its new body (no problems if the new body is wholly superior). If stuck in an inanimate object, with no source of fatigue, then it will have to use casting at -2 or worse (for having no fatigue), or something.

Final notes: a sandestin may be treated as a target, or bestowee, for other applications in an effect of which it is part; it may be allocated a controlled, bestowed application and bestow it upon another target. If affected by a magical effect, and appropriately equipped, a §17A4 or §17A5 sandestin may resist said effect (they would require the Incant skill). A sandestin may be attached to a prepared physical object or creature of sufficient size (GM's discretion as to what is sufficient; only one sandestin per object or creature); if the creature or object is destroyed, then the entire effect is lost. Otherwise, a sandestin is assumed to occupy the location of its invoker at the time it was invoked. A sandestin may be assumed to react with the speed of its invoker (ie. not instantaneously) once preconditions for actions are met.</p></section></div></div>
